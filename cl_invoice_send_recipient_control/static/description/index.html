Invoice emails only to who you chose

When you email an invoice, only the recipients you chose receive it, not everyone who follows the invoice.

Normally Odoo also sends a copy of an invoice email to the followers of the invoice. Switch on one setting per company and an invoice email reaches only the customer and anyone you add in the Send window. Messages written in the chatter keep working as usual.

What it does

🎯 Chosen recipients only

An invoice email reaches the customer and anyone you add when sending, and no one else.

👥 Followers left out

Followers of the invoice no longer get a copy automatically; one you add on purpose still does.

📤 Every standard send

It applies to Send on one invoice, Send on several invoices and automatic sending built on the same process.

⚙ One simple setting

Switch Invoice Emails: Skip Followers on or off per company in the Invoicing settings.

Built to behave well

  • If every recipient is removed in the Send window, nobody is emailed; followers do not get the invoice through the back door.
  • Messages written in the chatter still notify followers as usual.
  • Emails sent by other means, such as a template run by an automation, are not changed.
  • It filters Odoo's own recipient list instead of replacing it, so other apps that extend invoice notifications keep working.
