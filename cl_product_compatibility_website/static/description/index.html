No mismatched carts at checkout

Keeps online shoppers from paying for products that do not fit together, and tells them what to change.

When a cart holds products that do not fit together, such as a 12oz cup with a 16oz lid, the shop shows a notice on the cart naming each mismatch and the options that do fit. The Checkout button stays disabled and the checkout pages lead back to the cart until it is put right. It installs by itself as soon as Product Compatibility and eCommerce are both in place.

What it does

🛒 Notice on the cart

The cart lists each mismatch and the compatible products the customer can pick instead.

🚫 Checkout held back

The Checkout button is disabled, and the address and payment steps send the customer back to the cart.

💳 Payment refused

A cart with a mismatch cannot be paid, and that includes the express checkout buttons.

👀 Published options only

The alternatives suggested to shoppers are limited to products that are published in your shop.

Built to behave well

  • The notice follows the cart: it appears and clears as the customer changes quantities, without reloading the page.
  • A mismatch is still reported when there is no published product to suggest.
  • It relies on Odoo's own cart checks, so there are no custom checkout scripts to break when Odoo updates.
  • Nothing to configure: it uses the compatibility groups you already set up for your quotations.
