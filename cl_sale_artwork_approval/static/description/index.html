Approved artwork on every branded order

Customer logos are designed, approved and kept per customer and product, reused on the next order and passed on to production.

Tick Requires Artwork on the products you brand for customers. On the quotation, the salesperson requests artwork for a line, uploads the customer's logo and sends it to a designer. The designer uploads a proof, the salesperson approves it or asks for changes, and the approved design is used again automatically on that customer's next order.

What it does

🎨 Designer flow

The designer gets an activity, uploads the proof and sends it back; the salesperson then gets an activity to review it.

🔄 Rework or approve

Ask for changes with a comment or approve the proof; every proof submitted stays in the history.

📁 Per customer and product

Approved artwork is kept with a version number for each customer and product, or for a single variant.

♻️ Reused automatically

The next order for the same customer and product picks up the approved artwork, and New Version starts a changed design from it.

📋 Artwork tab

The order lists each line that needs artwork, the artwork it has and its status.

🏭 Sent to production

The manufacturing order shows the approved proof and gets a copy of the file attached.

Built to behave well

  • Only products marked Requires Artwork can hold back a confirmation, and customers who accept or pay online are never blocked.
  • Statuses change only through the buttons, and approved artwork can be neither altered nor deleted: a change means a new version.
  • The files on an artwork are private: only users who can open the artwork can open them, never portal users.
  • You choose in the settings whether missing approval blocks order confirmation and the start of production.
  • Needs only Sales and Manufacturing, with no Project, Timesheets or HR app.
