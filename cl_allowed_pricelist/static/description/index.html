Keep every customer on the right prices

Choose which pricelists each customer can be sold on, and quotations will accept no other.

Add the allowed pricelists on the customer form. From then on, a quotation for that customer only offers those pricelists, starts on one of them and refuses any other. Customers without a list work exactly as before.

What it does

📋 Allowed pricelists

Set the list once on the company and it holds for every one of its contacts.

🔍 Filtered choice

The Pricelist field on a quotation only offers the allowed pricelists, plus the one set on the customer itself.

🎯 Right from the start

A new quotation starts on an allowed pricelist, even when it is imported or the customer of a draft is changed.

🛡 Firm check

Saving a quotation on any other pricelist is refused, with a message naming the pricelists that are allowed.

🔎 Find restricted customers

A Restricted Pricelists filter in Contacts shows every customer that has a list.

🏢 Multi-company aware

Pricelists that belong to another company are ignored, so each company keeps its own rules.

Built to behave well

  • A customer with no list is not restricted at all.
  • Online shop orders keep the pricelist chosen by the website, so carts are never blocked.
  • Orders created by the system itself, such as integrations and scheduled jobs, are not blocked.
  • Only quotations are checked; confirmed and cancelled orders are left alone.
  • The field appears once the Pricelists option is switched on in the Sales settings.
