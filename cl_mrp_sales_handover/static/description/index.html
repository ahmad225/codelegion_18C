Keep sales in step with production

Delivery dates follow manufacturing, the salesperson is told when goods are handed over, and collection customers hear when their order is ready.

The sales order shows when its manufacturing will be ready and its delivery date follows along. When production hands the finished goods over to the warehouse, the salesperson gets a task to plan the delivery. Customers who collect their order receive a ready for collection e-mail, then reminders until they come.

What it does

📅 Delivery date in step

The delivery date of the sales order follows the planned end of its manufacturing orders, or is set when they are done. The latest one counts.

🤝 Agreed dates protected

A delivery date entered by hand is never moved silently: the salesperson gets a note, or it moves later only, as you choose.

📦 Handover to warehouse

A button on finished manufacturing orders. When all of them are handed over, the salesperson gets a Ready for Delivery task.

🎉 Customer event date

The date of the customer's event is shown to production, with a warning when manufacturing would finish too late.

📧 Ready for collection

Customers who collect their goods get an e-mail when their delivery order is ready, sent in the salesperson's name.

🔔 Collection reminders

Reminders go out every few days, up to the maximum you set, and stop once the order is collected or cancelled.

Built to behave well

  • A sales order counts as handed over only when every one of its manufacturing orders is. If a handover is undone, the salesperson is told.
  • Each delivery order and each backorder gets its own collection e-mail, sent once, and only when all its goods are really reserved.
  • A handover is set or undone with its buttons only. It cannot be typed or imported, and only manufacturing managers can undo one.
  • E-mails are queued instead of being sent in the middle of a validation, and a customer without an e-mail address is noted on the delivery order.
  • Every part can be switched on or off per company in the Manufacturing settings.
