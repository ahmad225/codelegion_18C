No more payments removed by accident

Asks for confirmation before a reconciled payment is removed from an invoice or bill.

In Odoo, removing a payment from an invoice takes a single click and is easy to do by mistake. With this app, clicking Unreconcile in the payment section of an invoice first opens a dialog that says exactly what will be removed, for how much and from which document. Confirm to go ahead, or choose Keep it and nothing changes.

What it does

⚠ Confirm before removing

Clicking Unreconcile in the payment section of an invoice opens a confirmation dialog first.

💬 A clear message

The dialog names what is removed, the amount and the document it is removed from, the right way round.

📄 All invoice types

Works on customer invoices, vendor bills, credit notes and receipts.

💡 Explains the effect

It reminds you that the amount becomes due again and that the payment itself is not cancelled and can be matched again later.

Built to behave well

  • It is only a confirmation step: the standard Reset to Draft button is never hidden or disabled, and nothing extra is stored on the invoice.
  • Once you confirm, the standard Odoo unreconcile does the work, exactly as before.
  • If the detailed message cannot be prepared, a general question is asked instead, so the dialog never blocks you.
  • Other ways of unreconciling, such as from journal items or bank reconciliation, are not changed.
