Statements your customers can rely on

Account and overdue statements as PDF, by email, on the customer portal and sent automatically every month.

Every statement is built from your posted accounting entries at the moment it is asked for, so it is always up to date. Print or email it for one customer or many, let customers view it on the portal, or have last month's statement sent to everyone automatically.

What it does

📄 Account statement

Any period, with the opening balance, every invoice, credit note and payment, a running balance and the closing balance.

⏰ Overdue and ageing

Open items past their due date with the days overdue, and ageing by due date from not due to over 90 days.

📧 Print or email

Send to one customer or a selection with the PDF attached, to the statement recipients you set per customer.

🌐 Customer portal

Customers open their own statement in My Account, filter it by date and download the PDF.

📅 Monthly sending

Last month's statement goes out automatically per company, from the day of the month you choose.

📝 Statements Sent log

Every statement that was sent, or not sent and why, is listed for follow-up.

Built to behave well

  • Nothing is stored per customer, so a statement is never out of date.
  • PDFs are private attachments, never public files, and portal customers only ever see their own statement.
  • Statements and the emails that carry them are worded in each customer's language.
  • Automatic sending gives each customer one statement per period, and an interrupted run carries on where it stopped.
  • A customer without an email address is logged as Not Sent and gets the statement automatically once an address is filled in.
