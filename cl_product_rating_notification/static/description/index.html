Hear about every product review

Emails the people you choose as soon as a customer rates or reviews a product in your online shop.

Pick who should be notified in the Website settings and you are done. Each new review sends them an email with the product, the customer, the star rating and the comment, plus a button that opens the review. If the customer later changes the rating or the comment, your team hears about that too.

What it does

⭐ New reviews

A new rating or review on a product emails your team straight away.

📝 Edited reviews

When a customer changes the rating or the comment, an update email goes out.

👥 Choose who hears

Pick the contacts to notify in the Website settings.

🏢 Per company

A product that belongs to a company notifies that company's team; otherwise the team of the website's company is notified.

📧 Your own template

A ready-made email is selected on install; edit its wording or choose another template.

🔗 Straight to the review

Each email has a button that takes your team to the review itself.

Built to behave well

  • Only product reviews notify; ratings on other records, such as tickets or tasks, are ignored.
  • One email per review, and saving a review without changing the rating or the comment sends nothing.
  • Emails are sent from your company's address, never from the reviewing customer.
  • A broken email template never stops a customer from posting a review.
  • Emails are queued, so the customer never waits for them to be sent.
