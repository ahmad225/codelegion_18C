Promotions applied as you quote

Applies the automatic promotions a quotation qualifies for whenever its lines change, and shows the customer what they saved.

Salespeople no longer have to click Reward on every quotation. Switch the option on in the Sales settings and free products and discounts are added as soon as a quotation qualifies. When several discounts compete, nothing is picked behind anyone's back: a banner asks the salesperson to choose.

What it does

🎁 Applied automatically

Free products and a single eligible discount are added as soon as the quotation qualifies.

👉 The salesperson chooses

When several discounts compete, a banner asks the salesperson to pick one in the standard rewards window.

⏰ End notice

One banner on the quotation tells when a promotion used on it is about to end or has already ended.

💰 Savings shown

A line under the totals of the order and the invoice, and on both PDFs, tells how much was saved through promotions.

📄 On the quotation PDF

The promotions applied are listed under the totals, with their end date while the quotation is still open.

📝 Changes logged

A short note in the order's history lists the promotions that were added or removed.

Built to behave well

  • Loyalty points, eWallet and gift card balances, coupons and discount codes are never spent or applied automatically.
  • Coupons, codes and rewards the salesperson applied by hand are never removed.
  • A promotion line the salesperson deletes stays off that quotation for as long as the promotion keeps applying.
  • Savings leave out eWallet and gift card payments, and each invoice shows only its own savings.
  • Online shop carts are left alone, since the shop applies its own promotions.
